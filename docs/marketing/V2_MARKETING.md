# DailySpend 2.0 — marketing kit

Copy, screenshot plans, and launch ideas for 2.0, in English and Simplified
Chinese. Everything here describes features in the 2.0 (16) build; check each
claim against the final build before publishing (see "Claims to keep true").

Character counts were measured for the fields that have App Store limits
(name and subtitle 30, promotional text 170, keywords 100, description and
What's New 4,000).

## 1. The idea in one line

**Snap it. Say it. Split it. See it.**
**拍一下，说一句，分得清，看得明。**

2.0 removes the typing from expense tracking. You scan a receipt, or say or
type one sentence, and DailySpend fills in the rest. Splitting with friends
works from the same receipt, and Insights tells you in plain words how this
month is going. All of it runs on the iPhone.

Four pillars, used everywhere below:

| Pillar | Feature | Proof point |
|---|---|---|
| Snap | Receipt scanning on Home | Amount, store, date, and category filled in; you confirm |
| Say | Quick Add, Siri, Shortcuts, Control Center, Action button | "Lunch with Alex 25" becomes a saved expense |
| Split | Scan into Quick Split or By Item | Tap who shared each line; tax and tip are divided for you |
| See | Insights quick look | "$219 less than this time last month" (same days, not the full month) |

Trust message under every pillar: **on your iPhone, no account, no ads.**

## 3. Claims to keep true

Check each one against the final build before it goes on the page.

| Claim | Why it's true today | Don't say |
|---|---|---|
| Receipts read on your iPhone, never saved or sent | Vision + on-device Foundation Models; images are transient | "AI-powered cloud scanning", "100% accurate" |
| No account, no ads | No sign-in; no ad SDKs | "No data leaves your phone" — optional iCloud sync does sync |
| DailySpend doesn't collect your data | App Privacy "Data Not Collected"; recheck the label for 2.0 | "Private by Apple" |
| Item scanning needs Apple Intelligence | `ReceiptIntelligenceStatus` gates item reading | "Works on every iPhone" for item scanning |
| Insights compares the same days | `SpendingPeriod.monthToDate` | "Compared with last month" (sounds like the full month) |
| Tax and tip divided to the cent | `Money` stores whole cents | — |

Apple names: write **Apple Intelligence**, **Siri**, **Shortcuts**, **Control
Center**, **Action button**, **Face ID**, **iCloud** exactly like this. Don't
use Apple logos, the Siri orb, or Apple device frames you don't have rights
to in ads. Apple's marketing guidelines allow describing compatibility in
words ("Works with Siri"). Don't suggest Apple endorses DailySpend.

## 9. Launch order

1. TestFlight first (see `docs/TESTER_DATA_WORKFLOW.md`, "Version 2.0
   TestFlight cycle"); fix what testers find.
2. Capture both screenshot sets from the build you submit, and recheck the
   App Privacy label.
3. Submit 2.0 with the new subtitle, keywords, description, and What's New.
   Set the promotional text and header asset.
4. After approval, release manually; check the public build on your own
   device over your existing data before telling testers to switch.
5. Start the In-App Event and post the launch messages the same day.
